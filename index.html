Atomic Networks · Polymarket Behavioral Cohort Analysis · May 2026

The atomic networks of Polymarket — how traders actually cluster, and where the bonds form.

Behavioral community detection on 59.2M trader–market edges across Polymarket's full history (Oct 2020 – Jan 2026, 1.98M wallets, 300K markets) surfaces 18 atomic networks — cohorts of traders bonded by shared trading behavior. The bonds between atoms (overlap in trader bases) reveal the actual audience structure of the platform. Time horizon splits crypto traders into five separate atoms on the same underlyings. Election eras are distinct atoms with measurable bonds across cycles. "Sports" splits into two. The Polymarket category tags don't see any of this — only behavior does.

Spotlight 1 · Time horizon splits crypto traders into five atoms

Five distinct atoms trade the same underlyings (BTC / ETH / SOL). They do share some traders — adjacent horizons bond more strongly (15-min BTC ↔ hourly BTC, Jaccard 0.23). But the populations are mostly distinct. Someone betting BTC at 15-min intervals is a different audience from someone betting BTC weekly price targets.

Spotlight 2 · Election eras are separate atoms — with bonds across cycles

Spotlight 4 · "Sports" is two distinct atoms with a measurable bond

Polymarket bundles all sports under one tag — but the wallet base is split. NFL + League of Legends bettors share some traders with NBA + soccer + boxing bettors (Jaccard 0.18), but the bond is moderate, not dominant. Two distinct audiences hidden under one platform category.

Methodology

Pipeline + key decisions that made the analysis honest.

Dataset: Jon-Becker/prediction-market-analysis — full Polymarket trade history scraped from Polygon (Oct 2020 – Jan 2026). 59.2M trader–market edges after extracting OrderFilled and FPMM events.

Filtering: Excluded 2 protocol contracts (CTF Exchange + NegRisk CTF Exchange) that appear as maker/taker in events but aren't real traders. Dropped 201 dust spammers (mean trade < $1, traded 1000+ markets) and 5,342 ultra-generalists (1000+ markets, blurs community boundaries). Singletons (1 market only) contribute no pairs.

Projection: Trader-market bipartite → market-market via Jaccard on shared traders (threshold ≥ 5 shared traders, Jaccard ≥ 0.01). Unweighted, not volume-weighted — answers "where do traders co-locate" not "where does capital co-flow."

Clustering: Louvain via igraph, weighted by Jaccard. Modularity 0.7838 — > 0.6 is generally considered well-separated.

Atomic bonds: For each atom-pair, count traders who participated in both. 153 atom-pairs measured; the network viz shows the top ~25 bonds (≥10K shared traders) — below that, bonds become statistical noise rather than meaningful audience overlap.
