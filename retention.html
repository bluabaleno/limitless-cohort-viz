Polymarket acquisition + retention by cohort · May 2026

Where new traders enter Polymarket — and who stays.

For each of the 18 cohorts, we computed two things: how many users entered Polymarket *through* that cluster as their first market (the acquisition funnel), and what fraction were still active 1, 3, 6, 12, 24 months later (the retention curve). The pattern is sharp: political cohorts both acquire and retain. Sports and crypto-horizon products acquire few users and lose almost all of them within months.

What this tells you

Strategic readthrough

  • Polymarket's growth machine is political news cycles. 82% of acquisitions through political cohorts AND those are the only cohorts that retain. Crypto and sports products are zero-acquisition surfaces.
  • Timing matters more than cluster. Within politics, users acquired *before* a news peak retain ~2× better than users acquired *during* the peak. Cluster 6 (Dem primary, pre-election) at 14.6% M+12 vs cluster 1 (election week) at 8%.
  • The 2020 election cohort is uniquely long-tail. 3.7% still active at M+24 — the only cluster with meaningful 24-month retention. Polymarket's OG audience that built habit during the original cycle.
  • 15-min and hourly crypto cohorts have catastrophic retention. Cluster 3 (15-min ETH/SOL): 0% return rate in M+1. Cluster 5 (hourly BTC/ETH): 7.8%. These are transactional venues for existing crypto users, not acquisition or retention engines.
  • Sports cohorts churn within seasons. Cluster 2 (NFL+esports) and Cluster 9 (NBA+soccer): essentially zero retention by M+6. Single-season tourists.
  • For Limitless's category-creation play: the analog is "acquire before a major event window, retain through it." Polymarket did this naturally via 2024 elections. A deliberate version requires choosing the right anchor event — and the user base built before the peak is more durable than the one acquired during it.
Methodology caveat: "Active in month M" comes from a market-span approximation — a trader is treated as active in every month between first_trade_ts and last_trade_ts of their markets. This overcounts months when a position was just being held without active trading. Real retention is likely lower than these numbers — directionally correct, magnitudes inflated. A rigorous version would require a raw-trades pass.
