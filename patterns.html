Polymarket trading-pattern analysis · May 2026

How the 18 cohorts actually trade — patterns by behavior, not just topology.

Per-cluster trading patterns across four dimensions: trade size, frequency, temporal cadence, and holding period. Three behavioral archetypes emerge clearly from the data — flash scalpers (hold positions for minutes), sustained news traders (hold for hours, return for weeks), and event-driven tourists (one-shot bets held until resolution). Each cluster falls into one of these patterns, and the patterns map nearly perfectly to the markets' own time horizons.

The three archetypes

Emerge directly from the data. Holding period — how long a position is held between first buy and first sell — is the cleanest single differentiator. Activity span and trade frequency confirm the pattern.

Per-cluster breakdown

Each card: top-line metrics + four behavioral lenses (trade size distribution, hour of day, day of week, monthly timeline). Cards are sorted by total volume. Color-coded by archetype.

Holding period methodology: for each (trader, outcome token), we computed time from first buy to first sell. Open positions (held to resolution) are tracked separately as "pct_closed". Activity span: from first to last trade per (trader, cluster). Trade size: per-trader-per-market average (total volume ÷ trade count), weighted by trade count in the histogram. Temporal: hour-of-day in UTC, day-of-week with 0=Sunday, monthly = year-month grouping.
